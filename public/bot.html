About KnotBot

KnotBot is the autonomous indexing crawler for Knot (“Find your people. Find your next event.”).

Purpose & Mission

KnotBot indexes upcoming public tech events, hackathons, and developer gatherings across India and online. Its sole purpose is discovery: helping developers, students, and founders find hackathons and sending participants directly to the official organizer registration websites without intercepting or gating applications.

Crawler Identification

KnotBot identifies itself with the following honest User-Agent string:

Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; KnotBot/1.0; +https://ne-x-gen-coder-s-syed-suhail-knot.vercel.app/bot)

Crawler Ethics & Politeness Policy

  • Robots.txt: KnotBot strictly obeys robots.txt rules on every target host.
  • Rate Limiting: Requests to any single domain are throttled with at least 1.5 seconds between fetches.
  • No Anti-Bot Bypassing: KnotBot never bypasses CAPTCHAs, paywalls, or authentication gates.
  • Read-Only: KnotBot performs strictly safe HTTP GET / HEAD requests.
  • Canonical Attribution: Listings always link directly back to the original source and organizer URL.

How to Control or Disallow KnotBot

To prevent KnotBot from indexing your site, add this to your robots.txt:

User-agent: KnotBot
Disallow: /

To disallow specific directories:

User-agent: KnotBot
Disallow: /private-events/
Disallow: /internal/
