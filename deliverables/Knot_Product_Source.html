HONEST BOUNDARY
  • Browser-local repository; no production cloud or multi-device sync.
  • No live platform connector or internet-scale crawl is claimed.
  • Local guardian attestation is not production-verified consent.
  • No email/SMS/WhatsApp delivery or proof of reading.
  • Paid ticketing, live video, WhatsApp automation and dependent profiles remain separately gated.
